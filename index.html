Austria's economy, mapped

Where Austria's companies are, from the whole country down to individual towns, and how the nine Bundesländer compare on output and income.

Where the companies are

Active companies in the Austrian commercial register, counted by where they are registered. Zoom in to go from states to districts, municipalities, places and finally each company's address, or click any area.

Source: justizonline (Firmenbuch) extract of active companies. Counts per area are based on postcode and place. Individual positions come from the official Austrian address register (© Österreichisches Adressregister, Stichtagsdaten vom 01.04.2026). Companies with a foreign or missing address are not mapped. Personal data of owners and managers is not shown.
