Austria Wirtschaft

Where Austria's companies are

Every active company in the justizonline commercial register extract, mapped from the whole country down to its street address.

Zoom in to go from states to districts, municipalities, places and finally each company's address, or click any area.

Companies with a foreign or missing address are not mapped. Personal data of owners and managers is not shown.
